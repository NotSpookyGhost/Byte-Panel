Invitations arrive as two emails: a welcome with the panel link and username, then a separate generated temporary password sent about 12 seconds later. Inbox arrival times may vary. Temporary passwords expire after 24 hours and must be changed before panel access. Chosen passwords are never emailed. Save these settings before testing.

Gmail: use smtp.gmail.com, port 587, STARTTLS and your full email address as the username. This panel uses password authentication, so use a Google app password with 2-Step Verification enabled, not your normal Google password.
